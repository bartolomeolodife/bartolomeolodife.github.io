    The project

    Product Design II examProf. Massimiliano DattiIdeas for Wood competition, Carpanelli and Tabù

    A project for Professor Massimiliano Datti’s Product Design II course, for the Ideas for Wood 2025/26 competition by Carpanelli, a fine cabinetmaking company, together with Tabù, a maker of precious wood veneers. The theme, “A New Touch of Elegance”, asked for a living-room piece with wood as its main material.

    A centuries-old tension system. The trabucco is an ancient fishing machine from the coast of Apulia: a wooden platform reaching out over the sea, balanced by cables and counterweights. The cabinet follows the same logic: the shelves extend from the central body, held by braided steel nautical cables stretched from the top to the end of each shelf. Steel clamps lock the cable at every shelf.

    Lightness and customisation. The inner structure is a honeycomb core enclosed by a perimeter frame, which reduces weight while keeping stiffness. Outside, the plywood is covered with Tabù veneers, chosen species by species. The cylindrical body hides a compartment reached through a sliding drawer.
