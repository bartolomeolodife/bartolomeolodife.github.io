How I work

Method and research are the thread that holds together everything I learn.

01

I learn design from every side.

I am training in many disciplines at once: product, visual communication, editorial design, exhibition design, photography, 3D modelling. I don’t see them as separate paths: I always look for a thread that holds them together.

02

Method above all.

From every discipline, the most important thing I take with me is method: a logical way of tackling a problem, which works for an object as much as for a book or an exhibition. It is what makes a project coherent from start to finish, where every choice has to hold up.

03

Starting from research.

For me every project begins with research. Before drawing I try to understand: the context, the people, the materials, the history. Form comes afterwards, and it has to answer what I have found.
